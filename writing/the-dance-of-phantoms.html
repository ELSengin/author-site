Poetry

The Dance of Phantoms

Originally published in Möbius: The Poetry Magazine — 2000.

Ice clung to the edges, Of this quiet little stream, And skating on its surface, Were strands of sun-raised steam. They danced across the water, Like white phantoms out to play, Put on a show before me, Then went drifting on their way. Their movements were hypnotic, As they wove between each bank, Not one rose off the water, Not a single phantom sank. They seemed to move with purpose, Silent beings of the creek, Intent upon their dancing, Kissing deadfalls on the cheek. They wove their spell in silence, They explored each bank in turn, And they let the breezes push them, As I watched their patterns churn. I watched this dance of phantoms, 'Til they drifted out of sight, Then I meandered homeward, In that magic winter's light. Enjoy your dancing phantoms, As you watch the scen'ry change, And go peaceful on your journey, Down that twisting stream you range.
