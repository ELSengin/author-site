Poetry

Something is Missing

Originally published in Möbius: The Poetry Magazine — 2000.

He can feel… but not identify the absence, a ghost that haunts the shadows of his thoughts; it’s there when looked at indirectly, so slippery when grasped it can’t be caught. He remembers friends… not just more friendly faces. From days when… there was more to lose than gain. Perhaps it was… their youth and lack of wisdom that let them care and trust more than is sane. Perhaps… it was the risk that drove them closer, the struggles… or the hardships that they shared; the chips collapsed… but they stood by each other, a thing that in the business world is rare. Friend is not a term he uses lightly, not with the standard that was set before; acquaintances are what his world now offers, but in him something whispers, “There was more….”
