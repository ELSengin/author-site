Poetry

Dead Center

Originally published in Möbius: The Poetry Magazine — 1999.

Dead center of his chest I aimed, And squeezed the trigger slow, And waited... devoid of feeling, For the hammer to let go. We'd been dispatched to this arms room, Two squad cars and their crews; Sent our partners to the corners, Where they'd wait for further news. Senior partners joined together— We had worked that way before; So in silence we moved forward To the arms room's open door. The alarm was not a false one, Arms room doors at night are locked, We exchanged a look of "Ready?!", And went in, both weapons cocked. Crates and boxes lay before us I went left and Nick went right To search the darkened arms room, Two more shadows in the night. A figure turned to face us, In his hand was something dark. My weapon—aimed and readied; Nick's command, a vicious bark. His hands moved upwards towards me. In slow motion I saw all; I watched it from a distance; All movements seemed to crawl. Dead center of his chest I aimed; I squeezed the trigger, slow, And waited... devoid of feeling, For the hammer to let go. The trigger traveled backwards At a slow and even rate; His hands were moving forward, But I knew he'd be too late. The trigger passed its center, Where the pressure drops to none, And it travels backwards faster, A hairs breadth ‘til it’s done. "Don't shoot!" screamed out the subject, His voice I heard at last, The trigger reached its fire point, I grabbed the hammer fast. His hands continued upward, 'Til above his head they stopped; A nightstick in his right hand, As soon as ordered, that, he dropped. I had planned to kill this person. The emotions finally came. A line was crossed inside me, And I'd never be the same. I'd gone past "If I have to." To "This man IS gonna die!" There was no hesitation. I was gonna kill that guy. My badge and gun weighed more now, Than they had when I came in. If I had been wrong this time, I could be wrong again. This moment long would haunt me. I would dream of it at night: I'd be forced to pull the trigger, But again might not be right. Dead center of his chest I aim, And squeeze the trigger, slow, And wait... devoid of feeling, For the hammer to let go.
