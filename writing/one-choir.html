One Choir

Originally published in Möbius: The Poetry Magazine — 2000.

I sat alone one evening, Far from the noise of Man, Just me, the frogs, and the crickets, As darkness enveloped the land. They sang—their music relaxing, A chorus that sang from the heart, On a knoll in the middle of nowhere I listened while each played his part. Each sang for its own reasons, In differing language and tone, Independent of all other voices, But never quite singing alone. Complex and subtle this chorus Beneath a million small suns, Connecting the one to the many, Condensing the many to One.
