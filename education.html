Engineering Product Development Curriculum

A seven-module engineering curriculum built around Synergistic Engineering Design — a structured methodology that connects product needs, engineering design, materials, part geometry, tooling, equipment, and manufacturing process to reliable production.

Synergistic Engineering Design treats product development as one connected engineering system rather than a series of independent activities. Reliable products depend on the interaction of design, material, tooling, equipment, and process. A decision made in one area changes the stresses, variation, manufacturability, or reliability requirements in another.

The curriculum follows that system from beginning to end: first defining what the product must accomplish, then developing and evaluating design concepts, identifying interdependencies and stresses, understanding potential failure mechanisms, and verifying that the design can survive its intended life. The later modules apply the same reasoning to plastic material behavior, part geometry, tooling and equipment, and finally the molding process that must reproduce the design consistently in production.

The goal is not simply to teach individual engineering tools. It is to show how those tools work together to move from an identified product need to a design that can be manufactured repeatedly and reliably.

Seven-Module Curriculum

Each module combines instructor-led education with a detailed companion volume developed to extend the course beyond the classroom. The companion materials expand the instructional content with engineering explanation, worked examples, practical reference material, case studies, and working engineering tools that can be used during actual product-development and manufacturing work.

Working Engineering Tools

The curriculum includes working engineering resources such as requirements and QFD matrices, Pugh-style decision matrices, material-selection worksheets, cooling and cycle-time calculators, clamp-force calculators, cost models, verification worksheets, troubleshooting guides, and other tools intended to support actual engineering work as well as instruction.

Companion Volumes & Technical References

The instructor-led courses are being supported by detailed companion volumes designed to serve both as course texts and as continuing engineering references. These written editions expand the classroom material with additional explanation, worked examples, calculations, case studies, and working engineering resources so the methodology can be applied after the course to actual product-development and manufacturing problems.
