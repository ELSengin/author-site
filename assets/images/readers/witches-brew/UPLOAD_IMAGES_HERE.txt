Upload consecutively numbered WebP reader pages here.

Examples:
Witches_Brew_Reader_001.webp
Witches_Brew_Reader_002.webp
Witches_Brew_Reader_003.webp
...

The reader automatically starts at 001 and continues until the first missing sequential image.
If only 001 through 005 are present, the reader displays 1 / 5 through 5 / 5.
If 001 through 028 are present, it displays 1 / 28 through 28 / 28.
Do not leave gaps in the numbering.
