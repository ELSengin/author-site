Engineering Education

Practical engineering education built around a comprehensive approach to product development.

The Engineering Product Development Curriculum follows the progression from defining what a product needs to accomplish through engineering analysis, material selection, plastic part design, tooling, and scientific injection molding. The courses are supported by engineering tools, worksheets, calculators, and reference materials developed for use on actual projects.
