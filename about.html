About Erick L. Sokn

Engineer, educator, and writer.

I grew up on a working farm in rural Illinois, served as a U.S. Army Military Policeman, worked in the trades and as a structural ironworker, and later spent more than three decades in engineering, product development, manufacturing, and engineering leadership.

The work was different from one stage to the next, but many of the same habits carried through: understand what is really happening, pay attention to what is slightly off, break difficult problems into manageable pieces, make the best decision the available evidence allows, and own the outcome.

Throughout my career, I developed an engineering approach by integrating structured problem solving, physics of failure, product development, manufacturing, and practical experience. Much of it became instinctive through years of application. Later, when asked to explain that approach to others, I organized it into a systematic methodology that could be taught and applied. Over time, that methodology evolved into the Engineering Product Development Curriculum presented on this site.

Today my work includes engineering consulting, engineering education, poetry, essays, memoir, and the development of technical reference materials based on decades of engineering experience.
