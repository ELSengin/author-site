Engineering Plastic Materials

This course teaches material selection as an application decision. Participants learn how time, temperature, moisture, chemicals, UV exposure, loading, strain rate, fillers, and reinforcements change the behavior of engineering plastics.

Core Topics

  • Amorphous and crystalline materials
  • Creep and stress relaxation
  • Temperature and moisture effects
  • Chemical resistance and environmental stress cracking
  • Fillers and reinforcements
  • Material-family tradeoffs and selection
