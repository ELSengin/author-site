Scientific Injection Molding

This course focuses on developing and maintaining repeatable cavity conditions. The goal is not simply to make acceptable parts, but to understand and control the variables that determine whether the process remains stable over time.

Core Topics

  • Material drying and moisture control
  • Melt temperature and residence time
  • Fill speed and transfer
  • Pack pressure and gate freeze
  • Cooling and cycle development
  • Process windows, troubleshooting, and repeatability
