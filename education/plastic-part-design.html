Plastic Part Design

Engineers experienced with metals often discover that plastics require a different approach. This course focuses on the specific design rules and material behaviors that determine whether a plastic part will work reliably and be manufacturable.

Core Topics

  • Wall thickness and coring
  • Ribs, draft, and fillets
  • Creep modulus and long-term loading
  • Shrinkage, sink, warpage, and weld lines
  • Snap fits and thermal expansion
  • Tolerancing and design for molding
