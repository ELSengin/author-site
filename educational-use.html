Educational Use & Engineering Responsibility

The engineering education, examples, worksheets, calculators, guidelines, and other tools provided through this site and its courses are intended for educational and professional-development purposes. They provide methods and tools to support engineering analysis and decision-making but do not replace the judgment, analysis, verification, testing, or review required for a specific application.

Engineering results depend on the particular design, materials, equipment, manufacturing processes, operating conditions, applicable standards and regulations, and other project-specific factors. Users are responsible for determining the suitability of the information and tools for their application, verifying calculations and assumptions, and ensuring that resulting designs, processes, tooling, equipment, and products meet applicable requirements.

Engineering problems may have more than one acceptable solution. Examples, guidelines, calculations, and design approaches presented in the educational material represent practical engineering methods but should not be interpreted as the only acceptable solution for every application. Product geometry, materials, tooling, equipment, manufacturing processes, supplier practices, operating conditions, and other requirements may require modification, engineering compromise, or an alternate approach.

Calculated or recommended values may represent engineering starting points that require application-specific evaluation, adjustment, and verification unless the particular method establishes the final value through measurement or verification on the actual system. Critical assumptions and results should be checked for the specific application.

Participation in a course or use of the provided materials does not guarantee a particular engineering, manufacturing, product, or business outcome.

Use of this website, educational materials, or course tools does not by itself create a consulting engagement or project-specific engineering responsibility. Any consulting services are governed by the scope and terms agreed upon for that engagement.
