Engineering Philosophy & Approach

Experience has taught me that successful engineering rarely depends on one discipline alone. The best solutions come from understanding how design, materials, manufacturing, tooling, process, and the people involved interact as a complete system.

Throughout my career, I developed an engineering approach by integrating structured problem solving, physics of failure, product development, manufacturing, and practical experience. Much of it became instinctive through years of application. Later, when asked to explain that approach to others, I organized it into a systematic methodology that could be taught and applied. Over time, that methodology evolved into the Engineering Product Development Curriculum presented on this site.
