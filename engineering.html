Engineering Philosophy & Approach

Experience has taught me that successful engineering rarely depends on one discipline alone. The best solutions come from understanding how design, materials, manufacturing, tooling, process, and the people involved interact as a complete system.

Throughout my engineering career, I used an approach that integrated product requirements, engineering design, project execution, structured problem solving, and the physics of failure with experience across product development, manufacturing, material selection, tooling, and processing. I eventually organized that into what I call Synergistic Engineering Design — a comprehensive product development approach that treats these disciplines as parts of one connected system. In various forms, the approach was taught and applied at two of the companies where I worked over a period spanning more than twenty years. It became the foundation for the Engineering Product Development Curriculum presented on this site.
